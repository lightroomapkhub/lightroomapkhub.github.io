About Lightroom APK Guide

Lightroom APK Guide is an independent, free resource for mobile photographers. We write practical guides about Adobe Lightroom — presets, portrait and landscape editing, color grading, masking, export settings and more — in plain language, for beginners and experienced editors alike.

Why this site exists

Search results for "Lightroom Mod APK" are full of download sites that promise premium features for free. Many of those files contain malware, steal data, or simply do not work — and they put your photos and your accounts at risk. We built this site to give honest answers instead: what a mod APK really is, what the risks are, what the official free version can already do, and how to get the real app safely.

What we do — and do not do

We publish editing tutorials and free in-browser tools. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored. We do not host, link to, or provide any APK file — modified or otherwise. The only download links on this site point to the official Adobe Lightroom app on Google Play and the Apple App Store.
